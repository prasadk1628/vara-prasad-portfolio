Philosophy

Data is just noise until you ask a question worth answering.

I'm not interested in dashboards for their own sake. I care about the moment when a chart makes someone in a room say "we didn't know that." That's the whole point.

My focus areas — behavioral analytics, engagement systems, infrastructure intelligence — share a common thread: they're all about understanding why people and systems behave the way they do, and what levers actually move outcomes.

🎯
Behavioral Intelligence
Measuring how users actually interact with systems, not how they say they do. Attention, friction, drop-off, loop formation.
📊
Insight-Driven Decisions
Analysis only matters when it changes what someone does next. Every project anchored to a concrete decision.
🔗
Cross-Domain Pattern Recognition
Electronics background meets CSE — I see IoT data streams and product metrics as the same problem in different clothes.
🌐
Public Good Analytics
Infrastructure and education data shouldn't sit in government databases. Visualization is an act of making invisible problems undeniable.
